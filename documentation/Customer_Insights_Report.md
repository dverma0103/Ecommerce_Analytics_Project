### Key Findings

# 7. Customer Acquisition Trends

## Monthly Customer Registrations

Customer registration remained consistent throughout the 18-month analysis period.

### Notable Observation

* March 2024 recorded the highest customer acquisition volume with 178 registrations.
* Customer acquisition remained stable across most months.
* November 2024 contains only partial-month data and should not be directly compared with previous months.

### Business Insight

The business demonstrates sustainable customer acquisition with no major fluctuations or prolonged declines in registration activity.

---

# 8. Strategic Recommendations

### Customer Retention

* Implement loyalty programs for high-value customers.
* Offer personalized discounts for repeat purchasers.
* Develop retention campaigns targeting customers with declining purchase frequency.

### Customer Reactivation

* Launch win-back campaigns targeting inactive customers.
* Use email and app notifications to encourage re-engagement.

### Customer Growth

* Continue acquisition initiatives that drove strong growth during March 2024.
* Analyze marketing channels contributing to customer registrations and optimize investment accordingly.

---

# 9. Conclusion

Customer analytics reveals a balanced customer base, strong repeat purchase behavior, and healthy revenue contribution across all customer segments. Regular customers currently contribute the highest revenue, while a repeat customer rate of 59.68% indicates strong retention performance.

Future business growth should focus on increasing customer lifetime value, improving retention strategies, and reactivating inactive customers to maximize long-term revenue generation.
