# Marketing Insights Report

## Executive Summary

The marketing analysis evaluates campaign effectiveness, channel performance, customer acquisition efficiency, and return on advertising investment (ROAS) across 18 months of business operations.

Key findings indicate that marketing activities generated **₹32.19 Million** in revenue from **₹16.32 Million** in advertising spend, achieving an overall **ROAS of 1.97x**. Among all campaigns, the **Referral Program** delivered the strongest overall performance, while **Email Marketing** emerged as the highest-performing marketing channel.

# Key Business Recommendations

1. Increase investment in Referral Programs due to their superior revenue generation and ROAS.
2. Expand Email Marketing campaigns as they provide the highest channel-level efficiency.
3. Continue using New User Discounts for customer acquisition while optimizing campaign costs.
4. Improve performance during low-ROAS months through audience segmentation and targeted promotions.
5. Allocate additional budget toward high-performing channels and campaigns while reducing spend on lower-efficiency initiatives.

---

# Final Conclusion

The marketing ecosystem demonstrates strong performance with a healthy overall ROAS of 1.97x. Referral Programs, Email Marketing, and App Push Notifications emerge as the most effective strategies for balancing customer acquisition, revenue generation, and marketing efficiency. Future optimization efforts should focus on improving campaign profitability while maintaining conversion volume.
