# Executive Summary

### Key Strengths

* Strong customer retention with a Repeat Customer Rate of 68.69%.
* Balanced revenue contribution across customer segments.
* Marketing campaigns consistently generate positive returns with an overall ROAS of 1.97.
* Pharmacy and Pet Care categories exhibit healthy revenue-to-risk ratios.

### Key Risks

* Inventory Damage Rate remains critically high at 54.41%.
* Household Care category experiences the highest operational losses.
* Approximately 30% of orders are delivered late.
* New customers face the highest delivery delay rates, potentially affecting retention.

### Strategic Recommendations

1. Reduce inventory damage through improved warehouse handling and stock monitoring.
2. Prioritize delivery performance improvements for first-time customers.
3. Focus future growth initiatives on high-performing categories such as Dairy & Breakfast, Pet Care, and Pharmacy.
4. Optimize marketing attribution models to better align reported revenue with realized sales.
5. Monitor high-revenue/high-damage products and implement targeted inventory controls.

---

## Conclusion

The business demonstrates strong customer retention, stable revenue generation, and positive marketing performance. However, inventory losses and delivery inefficiencies present significant opportunities for operational improvement. Addressing these areas can improve profitability while enhancing customer satisfaction and long-term growth.
