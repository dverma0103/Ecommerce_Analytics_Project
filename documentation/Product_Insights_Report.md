# 8. Inventory Health Analysis

## Potential Inventory Risk Products

| Product        | Min Stock Level | Total Stock Received | Total Damaged Stock | Net Stock Received |
| -------------- | --------------: | -------------------: | ------------------: | -----------------: |
| Detergent      |              30 |                  295 |                 324 |                -29 |
| Toilet Cleaner |              23 |                  292 |                 300 |                 -8 |
| Dish Soap      |              15 |                  330 |                 338 |                 -8 |

### Key Findings

* Three products were identified as potential inventory risk items.
* Damaged stock exceeds total stock received for all three products.
* Negative inventory values suggest possible data quality issues or limitations within the inventory dataset.

### Business Insight

Detergent, Toilet Cleaner, and Dish Soap should be monitored closely for replenishment requirements. However, inventory calculations should be validated before making operational decisions.

### Data Limitation

The inventory dataset does not contain current stock balances or sales-based stock deductions. Inventory risk was estimated using:

Net Stock Received = Total Stock Received − Total Damaged Stock

Results should therefore be interpreted as potential inventory risk indicators rather than actual stock shortages.

# 11. Strategic Recommendations

### Product Strategy

* Increase focus on top-performing products such as Vitamins, Pet Treats, and Cough Syrup.
* Expand offerings within high-revenue categories.

### Inventory Optimization

* Investigate causes of excessive damaged stock.
* Establish category-specific inventory monitoring based on shelf-life characteristics.

### Profitability Improvement

* Promote high-margin categories such as Instant & Frozen Food and Pet Care.
* Combine high-volume products with high-margin products through bundle promotions.

---

# 12. Conclusion

The product portfolio demonstrates balanced category performance with Dairy & Breakfast emerging as the highest revenue-generating category. Vitamins, Pet Treats, and Cough Syrup are the strongest product-level revenue contributors, while Instant & Frozen Food delivers the highest profitability margins.

Inventory damage and inventory risk indicators reveal opportunities for operational improvement. Future growth should focus on optimizing inventory management, improving product availability, and expanding high-margin product categories.
