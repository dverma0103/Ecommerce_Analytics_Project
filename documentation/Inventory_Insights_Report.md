# Inventory Analytics Report

## Project: E-Commerce Performance Analytics

### Objective

The objective of this analysis is to evaluate inventory performance, stock damage, inventory efficiency, and operational risks to identify opportunities for reducing wastage and improving inventory management.

---

# 8. Data Quality Observations

### Inventory Data Limitations

Several products identified during inventory analysis exhibit damaged stock quantities exceeding received stock quantities.

Examples include:

| Product        | Net Inventory |
| -------------- | ------------: |
| Detergent      |           -29 |
| Toilet Cleaner |            -8 |
| Dish Soap      |            -8 |

### Observation

Negative inventory balances are not operationally feasible in real-world inventory systems and indicate:

* Synthetic dataset limitations
* Missing inventory movement records
* Potential aggregation inconsistencies

### Analyst Note

These findings were documented and retained within the project to demonstrate data validation and quality assessment practices.

---

# 9. Strategic Recommendations

### Inventory Optimization

* Investigate root causes of inventory damage in Household Care and Personal Care categories.
* Establish tighter quality-control procedures for high-risk products.

### Product Monitoring

* Closely monitor Dish Soap, Detergent, and Toothpaste due to exceptionally high damage rates.
* Prioritize replenishment planning for products with recurring inventory issues.

### Operational Improvements

* Improve storage and handling processes.
* Implement inventory audits for categories with damage rates exceeding 60%.

---

# 10. Conclusion

Inventory analysis revealed significant operational inefficiencies, with an overall inventory damage rate of 54.41%. Household Care and Personal Care categories exhibited the highest damage rates, while products such as Dish Soap and Detergent emerged as the highest-risk inventory items.

The analysis highlights opportunities to improve inventory management, reduce stock losses, and enhance operational efficiency through better inventory controls and monitoring practices.
